**Your PRs sorted by what's next, a merge box that only offers what can work, and much less to read.**

**Your PRs**
- **Sections by what's next**: Ready to merge, To fix, Waiting (on reviewers, checks, auto-merge or the merge queue), Need reviewer, Draft.
- **To fix** holds what's yours to fix: changes requested, failing checks, conflicts, behind its base, refused by the merge queue, or required checks that never ran (a path or branch filter skipped them). The tab counts them in red; its hover says which.
- **Ask its session to fix it**: a To fix PR opened from a Codex or Claude Code session can hand it back, with what's in the way (the failing checks' log ends, what reviewers asked for).
- **A notification when your PR gets conflicts** (Settings → Notifications), and the menu bar names what holds your PRs up.

**Merging**
- **Only what can work is offered**: no auto-merge on conflicts or on required checks that never ran (it would wait for ever); "Then merge it" is off, saying why, when approving can't end in a merge.
- **Merge as admin in one step** when only an admin can merge.
- **Merged and closed PRs** say so once, in the tag in the bar: details on hover, Revert, Reopen, Delete branch and moving tickets in its menu.

**Reviewing**
- **Files lgtm hides or collapses are marked viewed at once**: generated, no content change, whitespace- or imports-only, archived specs, deleted.
- **Viewed marks** (Settings → Reviewing): shown, hidden on small PRs, or hidden.
- **Finish review** is green only with something unsent, with Discard review beside Submit; no strip repeating it.
- **Codex conversations are archived** when you approve or merge their PR, with Undo (Settings → Reviewing).

**Clearer**
- **Much less text**: about 80 notices, strips, toasts, confirms and pill headings cut to what you act on.
- **Icons that don't mislead**: "asked" and waiting show a clock, running and queued checks a dot, auto-merge the merge-queue icon; no ✓ that reads as approved or passed.
- **Text readable in every theme**, light and dark, and nothing cut off without its … at large text or in a narrow window.
- **Bots by their own name**, with a bot mark.
- **Videos play in the Mac app**, and pictures and videos whose GitHub address ran out load again.
