**A visual polish pass: buttons that look like buttons, one scale for sizes and corners, and panels that say what's in the way and what to do about it.**

**Look**
- **Not all white in light themes**: the top and review bars in the theme's panel colour; buttons filled, with an edge that shows, a step darker on hover and pressed; in dark mode, a step lighter than what they sit on.
- **macOS's accent colour** for links, focus and what's selected (Settings → Appearance), darker or lighter where it wouldn't read.
- **Green buttons with white text** in every theme, their green darkened until it reads.
- **One scale**: text in 11, 12, 13 and 14px (16, 20, 28 for titles), corners of 4, 6 or 10px, all following the text size you set.
- **Every bar button with an icon**: Merge, Finish review, Comment, and the ⋯ menu's actions.

**Merging**
- **The merge box a row per reason it can't merge**, each with its action: Update branch, Ask reviewers, Re-run failed, Turn off auto-merge.
- **Approved and Draft tags in the bar**: the Draft tag's menu has Ready for review (no draft banner).
- **Approvals counted as the repo's rules count them**: a bot's approval shows under Reviewers.

**Reviewing**
- **Finish review's notes as rows**, with See what changed, Look over and Go to the first.
- **Forms as macOS has them**: Cancel, then the main button on the right; a confirm's question bold, its button red when it can't be undone.
- **The PR's pills in groups**: where it stands, findings, what's in it, how big; every pill one height.
- **⌘K rows show their kind as an icon**, actions with their key on the right.
- **Toasts with a plain Undo and ×**; What's new and Welcome with their buttons at the bottom.

**Steadier**
- **No layout jumps** when a PR loads (the skeleton is in the page's own order) or the list refreshes.
- **Threads inside rendered Markdown files** keep lgtm's look, not the document's.
- **Needs Safari 17.4 or later**: an older web engine gets a message saying to update, not a half-styled app.
