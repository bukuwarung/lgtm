**Reviewing that doesn't wait on GitHub, your own threads on their own, and Browse.**

**Nothing waits**
- A review sends as you move on: the next PR opens at once. If sending fails, **Back to it** returns you with your comments and summary kept.
- Replies and resolves show at once, on the PR page and in the inbox, and are put back with a message if GitHub says no.
- Submit review shows what it's doing (Submitting…, Messaging on Slack…, Opening the next PR…).
- New comments go into your pending review on the site as you write them, so they're safe even if the app's data goes.

**Approve, and more from the PR page**
- **Approve can merge too**: Then merge it, or auto-merge while checks run, and it says why when it won't. **A** opens Finish review on Approve.
- **Take a team review** (T): a review asked of your team becomes yours by name, so the rest of the team stops waiting on it.
- **Update branch** where you can see it: a "behind develop" pill, the ⋯ menu and **U**. The PR reloads once the updated branch is there.
- Ready for review in the draft banner; Change base…, Ask QA (q), close, reopen and convert to draft in the ⋯ menu. A closed PR's branch can be restored and the PR reopened.
- The PR's labels in the index.

**Your threads** (y)
- A PR's threads you started or replied in, on their own, each with the lines it's on and what changed since. Waiting on you first, resolved ones folded; a file name jumps back to the diff.
- **Look them over**: Finish review opens it whenever there's something to send. What you're about to send comes first: your drafts and your agent's, with their lines. **Drop N outdated** clears comments whose code changed since.
- **Fixed in abc1234**: on your PR, a reviewer's thread offers the commit that changed its lines; one click replies and resolves.
- Finish review warns when commits landed while you reviewed, with See what changed.
- A review request's bell notice goes once you've answered it, here or anywhere.

**Code where comments are**
- Suggested changes read as diffs, not tables: in threads, the conversation and the inbox.
- The lines above a thread and a draft are drawn as the diff draws code: line numbers, colours, the commented lines marked.
- Code names in review comments link to the code they name, previewed on hover.
- **Where it's used** (Settings → Diff, on): point at a function or class the PR declares (or press **u**) for every line of the PR naming it, the specs naming it, and a search of the whole repo.
- Comment boxes complete code names after a backtick, #PRs and Jira keys.

**Big PRs**
- **Files you own**: when CODEOWNERS gives you some of a PR's files, by name or through a team, a pill says how many ("31 of 675"). Its menu lists them, and **Show only these** (o) cuts the page to them.
- Files draw nearest to the screen first, so a fast scroll doesn't land on blank files.
- **t** hides the diff's review threads while you read the code through (ones waiting on you stay).
- Resolved and outdated threads take one line, in the diff and the conversation. A review whose comments are all settled folds to one line, and approvals in a row read as one ("Approved by budi, rina and ana").
- Bots' comments group by bot, one line each.

**Agent reviews**
- Follow the repo's own rules: CLAUDE.md, AGENTS.md, REVIEW.md and copilot-instructions.
- Don't repeat what's been said, and run once per PR, not on every launch.
- Triage the agent's drafts from the keyboard (r; ↵ keep, e edit, ⌫ drop); a dropped draft can be put back.

**Browse** (was Team)
- A team, a repository or a person, picked through one switcher like GitHub's branch picker: your teams and recent repos first, anything else by typing. **Shift+B** opens it.
- A repo's open PRs grouped by where they stand.

**In the app**
- Images open in Preview (all of a comment's pictures together; a changed image's old and new side by side), videos in your video player.
- Right-click menus on PRs in the list, lines, file headers, comments and pictures.
- **Gists open in lgtm**: from a link, the search box or lgtm://gist/<id>. A patch reads as a diff, markdown is rendered, and anything else is highlighted.
- ⌘K does actions by name, and opens gist links. ⌘F remembers your last 10 searches.
- Dismiss a PR from the list (× on hover) until something new happens on it, with Undo.
- Local builds keep their macOS permissions (signed with a certificate in your keychain).

**Also**
- Settings → Slack → Find also tries the emails on people's commits; Settings → Accounts names a missing gh scope and the command to add it.
- Keys: f opens Finish review, J/K the next and previous PR in the list.
- A bot's face is its app's own picture.
