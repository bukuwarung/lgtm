**Slack where the review happens, reverts, and less to read.**

**Asking for review on Slack** (each in Settings → Slack, off to start with, with its preview)
- **Ask in channel** posts your review request to your review channel and tags each reviewer asked on the PR by name, so it's someone's job and not the whole channel's. You read and can edit it first.
- **Replies to your review request**: your post that links the PR shows beside its title, with how many replies you haven't opened.
- **Ask again in your review request's thread**: asking a reviewer to look again replies in that thread and tags them, instead of a DM.
- **People you asked on Slack, not as reviewers**: whoever you DM'd your PR to but GitHub or GitLab doesn't ask gets a pill to ask them there too.
- **✅ on review requests**: approving a PR puts a ✅ on its author's review-request post in a channel; your own posts get one when you merge.

**Asked of you on Slack**
- **PRs asked of you on Slack** join "Needs your review": a PR or MR linked in a DM to you, or next to a tag of you, in the last 7 days, when GitHub or GitLab doesn't ask you yet. Its pill adds you as a reviewer.
- **Answer in the thread where you were asked**: the message after your review replies under the message that asked you, tagging them.

**No one named, no one's job**
- A PR that asks only a team, or a GitLab approval rule with none of its approvers asked, gets a pill offering the team's least busy member.

**Reverts**
- **Revert** on a merged PR opens a PR that undoes it, on GitHub and GitLab, with an optional reason in its description. It can also reply in the reverted PR's review-request thread on Slack.
- A revert says whether it exactly undoes its PR, or which files differ. A clean revert skips the test warnings.
- A merged PR says when it's been reverted ("Reverted by #588, merged 2 weeks ago").
- A notice on the bell and on the desktop when someone opens a revert of one of your PRs, on GitHub and GitLab.
- Merging a revert moves its Jira tickets back to In Progress.

**Jira**
- **Move the Jira ticket when you ask for review** (Settings → Reviewing, off): Ready for review, or asking or re-asking a reviewer, moves the PR's tickets to In Review, or the status you pick.

**Late for them**
- Slack messages to someone it's late for go out at 9 am their time, scheduled on Slack (with an app sign-in). Weekends count as late, and swaps prefer people on a workday.
- Your PR's away-reviewer pill asks someone free instead, from the PR list too. A code owner is swapped for another owner of the changed files.

**People**
- **Real names** (Settings → Accounts, off): profile names where logins show. **Use Slack names** and **Pictures from Slack** fill in what GitHub profiles lack.
- **Shorter logins** (off): the ending your company's logins share is left off (jane_acme shows as jane).
- People are matched on Slack by themselves when their GitHub or commit email is exactly one Slack account.
- Slack is checked when you open Finish review, so a missing match shows before you submit.
- PRs show who they're assigned to, and a bot's PR leads with its assignee.

**Less to read**
- How much less there is to read, as a share of the PR ("45% less to read"), counting folded comments too. The start page adds up what lgtm kept out of your way, as reading time.
- Code moved without changes folds to one row naming where it went.
- Threads opened by agents (a comment starting with 🤖) fold under one line, unless one waits on you. A finding posted again is one line pointing at the first.

**Getting around**
- The search box shows where you are, the way Safari's address bar does. ⌘K selects the PR's link.
- Back keeps the recently viewed PRs: hold it or right-click it for the list (View → Recently Viewed, ⌘Y).
- Right-clicking a link offers Open in Browser and Copy Link.

**Faster, and lighter on GitHub**
- Opening a PR asks GitHub once instead of five times. The PR list costs 3 points a refresh instead of 32. The Team tab handles teams with hundreds of open PRs.
- Files, teams, reviewers, code owners and people are kept between launches. Settings → About → Refresh saved lookups clears them.
- Fewer whole files are read for the Room and JPA checks (44 reads on a big Android PR became 7).

**Also**
- Dotfiles and tool-named files (Jenkinsfile, .nvmrc, CODEOWNERS…) sort under Build and config.
- Private-repo images on branches with a slash load, and so do a picture's dark-mode variants.
- lgtm-links 0.5.0: Open in lgtm under any reply naming a PR as owner/repo#n.
