**Settings that make sense, things that stay put, and fixes found in the error log.**

**Settings**
- Reorganised: Notifications, Conversation, Coding agents, Slack and Jira are sections of their own, and every hint is cut to what the label doesn't already say.
- **Ignore whitespace stays on.** The pill, **w** and "less to read" switch it for the open PR only; before, a stray **w** or click turned it off everywhere.

**On the page**
- **Checkboxes drawn by lgtm**: bigger, in your theme's colour, and as clear when the window isn't focused.
- More checks mark their line in the diff: tests, CI or checks switched off, protections off, focused tests and debugger, raw HTML, `@Transactional` on a private method. Red or amber, the reason on hover.
- Icons centred in their buttons, everywhere.
- Pill menus near the window's edge open inward instead of running off it.
- A person's clock shows their GMT offset (GMT+7), not a city that's often wrong.

**When something fails**
- The red bar names the PR it's about, linked: after a review sent in the background you're usually on the next one.
- Approve and merge says what's blocking it (checks running, auto-merge not available) instead of "it can't merge yet".
- lgtm keeps its last 50 errors across restarts, for Copy Diagnostics.

**People**
- Accounts GitHub says aren't people (an automation bot) are treated as bots: no Slack messages, their comments fold with the other bots'. **Not a bot** in Settings → Slack → People undoes it.

**Keyboard and typing**
- The comment box's toolbar (Bold, Link, Suggest, Fix…) works from the keyboard.
- Typing Japanese, Chinese or Korean with an input method: Enter confirms your character instead of submitting, in every lgtm text box.
- Confirmations that can't be undone (deleting a branch or comment, discarding your review) start on Cancel.
- The Ask QA, ask-for-review and Change base dialogs no longer close when you click their edge.

**Also**
- Tested in WebKit, the Mac app's own engine, instead of Chrome.
