**The rest of GitHub's PR page, in lgtm: commits one at a time, reactions, editing what you sent, opening PRs, and answers that stream.**

**Reading a PR**
- **Each commit on its own**: the commits pill lists them; pick one to see just its changes, read-only. **<** and **>** step through them.
- **New comments and replies show up while the PR is open**, no reload; a toast says who.
- The description's task list ticks, by anyone who may edit it, and people with write access can edit the description too.

**Comments**
- **Reactions** on the description, comments, reviews and threads, on GitHub and GitLab: a smiley in each header, **+** for 👍 on the one under the mouse.
- **Edit or delete what you sent** from a comment's ⋯ (**E** edits the one under the mouse). A review's text can be edited, not deleted.
- **Hide a comment** as off-topic, outdated, resolved and so on, or unhide it, and **dismiss a review** that's blocking the merge (GitHub). **H** opens the ⋯ of the comment under the mouse.

**The PR's details**
- The index's **Details**: assignees (**G**), milestone (**M**), your notifications (**S**) and the conversation lock (**O**), on GitHub and GitLab.

**Opening PRs**
- Branches you pushed in the last three days with no PR yet wait above Your PRs, and in ⌘K. **Open a PR…** fills the title from the commit (or the branch), the description from the repo's template, and takes the base and draft or not; ⌘↵ opens it.
- A description's preview, when you edit one or open a PR, is in your Markdown style.

**Coding agents**
- **Answers stream in**: Ask shows Claude's answer as it's written, and an agent review's pill counts its findings as they come (reviewing… 2 found). Codex's answers still arrive whole.
