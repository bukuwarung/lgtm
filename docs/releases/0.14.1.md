**Commits as a list in the index, re-reviews that skip what came in from a rebase, Finish review at the end of the page, and a quieter index and PR list.**

**Commits**
- **The index's Commits section** (its top-row button) lists every commit; pick one to see just it: its message and files, with the description, conversation and ticket out of the way.
- **Shift-click** for a run of commits together. The ones you've opened are ticked, and **>** skips them. Commits pushed since your last review are marked; **C** opens them together.
- **Comment while reading a commit**: the comment goes on the same lines in all changes, while they're unchanged since.
- **Revert…** (**Z**), on your PR, undoes the commit on show with a new commit on the branch.
- Squash-merging, **From the commits** puts their headlines in the message (⌥C).

**Reviewing**
- **Re-reviewing after a rebase** shows only what the author changed, not what came in from the base branch.
- **Finish review is also at the end of the page**, open as you scroll to it; it's the same summary as the bar's. **Then mark the rest viewed** marks the files you skipped as it sends.
- **Mark files viewed as you scroll past them** (Settings → Reviewing, off), with Undo; **V** goes to the next file not marked viewed.
- **Quote reply** (**Q**): select text in a comment to quote it in a reply. **Reply and resolve** with ⌘⇧↵. An unsent reply is kept until you send it, and the PR list counts it.
- On your PR, the threads later commits fixed can all be answered at once: "Fixed in …" and resolved (**F**).
- Where you're mentioned: the index lists the comments that mention you, and your name stands out in them.
- A changed image also **fades** over the old one, or shows its **difference**: unchanged black, changes bright.

**Reviewers**
- One line each: the name and their Slack status's emoji, with how many PRs wait on them; the rest on hover.
- Asking someone who's away, busy or where it's late asks you first.
- **Undo** right after asking, or **×** on a reviewer who hasn't answered, takes the request back, and the Slack message lgtm sent them: deleted, or emptied to "-", or, when the workspace allows neither, answered in its thread.

**A quieter index and PR list**
- **Hide empty sections in the index** (Settings → Reviewing, on): labels, assignees, milestone and checks with nothing in them leave; icons at the top of the index add them.
- **Hide empty groups** in the PR list (Settings → PR list, off): "Nothing needs your review" in one line instead.

**Fixes**
- The index's search boxes no longer run past its edge, and a click elsewhere closes them.
- Checkboxes sit level with their words in Finish review, the merge box and Settings.
